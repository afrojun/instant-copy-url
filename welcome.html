Finish setting up your shortcut

Chrome may leave an extension shortcut unassigned when that key combination is unavailable. Chrome requires you to choose it yourself.

  1. Open Chrome’s extension shortcut settings.
  2. Find Instant Copy URL and click the pencil.
  3. Press the shortcut shown on the left and leave it set to In Chrome.

Chrome could not open the page. Enter chrome://extensions/shortcuts in the address bar.

This extension cannot assign or replace shortcuts on your behalf.

Optional · macOS

Move tabs between Chrome profiles

Install ProfileBar, then right-click a page (or a tab in Chrome 150+) and choose Move tab to profile. Choose Enable ProfileBar integration… from that menu, allow local access when Chrome asks, then pick a profile. Your copy shortcut works without ProfileBar.
